Why this project exists

Make a Chog NFT playable.

From static collectible to a character with a reason to return.

Utility that starts with play.

Chog Playverse is an independent community prototype exploring how Chog Genesis can become more than a profile picture: the character becomes part of an interactive game world. Players return for skill-based runs, daily Chog knowledge, personal milestones and collection-aware identity.

Why Chog? Chog's playful, expressive character is a natural fit for a game-first community experience. NFT ownership could eventually support character-specific visuals, unlockable challenge modifiers and collectible progression—without making basic play paywalled.

NOWPlayable games, daily quiz, read-only NFT checker, local scores and XP.
NEXTMetadata-based character traits, more missions and polished audio/FX.
FUTUREVerified global leaderboard, account sync and community events—if a backend is added.

Independent community prototype. Not an official Chog product or endorsement. XP is a local game-progress score only; no tokens are issued, no prizes are promised, and no on-chain transactions are made.

Quick answers

Questions about Chog Playverse.

Tap a question to see the answer. Clear details about the Chog focus, NFT checker and what this prototype actually does.

CHOG PLAYVERSE is an independent, community-built arcade concept inspired by Chog Genesis on Monad. It combines three playable game modes, a daily knowledge quiz and a read-only NFT checker in one responsive experience.
The Chog collectible is the visual identity and the starting point for future character utility. A next version could use verified NFT metadata to map traits to skins, optional abilities, special missions and seasonal challenges. This prototype does not yet assign unique in-game abilities to individual token IDs.
Wallet connection is optional. You can connect an EVM wallet from the header to show your address and switch to Monad, but the site never requests a signature or transaction. You can also play and check NFT ownership without connecting by pasting a public Monad address. RPC availability can affect the result.
Not yet. Scores and quiz points are stored in your browser on this device. A real global leaderboard needs a backend, anti-cheat validation and a clear privacy policy. The interface does not fabricate global player rankings.
No. The demo has no token rewards, NFT mint, paid entry or guaranteed prizes. The token contract link is provided only as a reference and should be verified independently before any trade.
